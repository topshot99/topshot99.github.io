# Building Open-Source Video AI Locally: Starting the Journey

I've recently gone down the rabbit hole of **Video Analytics** and **Language-based Video Q&A** after reading about companies like TwelveLabs and others building in this space.

The more I explored, the more I realized this isn't just about a powerful multimodal model. It's a complete engineering pipeline:

- Scene detection
- Keyframe extraction
- Speech recognition
- Motion understanding
- Embeddings
- Vector search
- Temporal retrieval
- An LLM on top

Now I'm wondering:

> **Can I build a simplified version locally using only open-source models and tools?**

That's what I'm going to explore next.

This is the starting point for a series of posts where I'll document everything I learn—from architecture and design decisions to experiments and failures—one component at a time.

If you're also interested in Video AI, multimodal systems, or Video RAG, I'd love to connect and learn together. Feel free to share papers, projects, or open-source repositories that you think are worth exploring.
